V4.36, the default main URL, and the unrelated 28-board sports project remain unchanged.

This opt-in view creates a rotating three-person photographic showcase, built
exclusively from the four previously source-reviewed WWE wrestler photographs
by Diego Serrano under CC BY 2.0. These are real photographs, not AI
lookalikes. The image source is the 2025 Royal Rumble; photos do not imply
2026 clothing, titles, rankings or current champions.

Only names in the exact date-matched upcoming match card are eligible;
managers listed in (w/...) are excluded, champion labels removed for name
matching, and no appearances are invented. Images that cannot be matched
are never substituted with a different wrestler. Every card stays labelled
ON REPORTED CARD. All four photos are available through the rotation.

If fewer than two eligible photos match, the visual enhancement disables
itself rather than showing old wrestlers under a new date.

The SmackDown show mark is the existing trademarked identifying logo.
The master Man Cave logo is NOT claimed approved: the rejected standalone
checkerboard logo remains excluded, while metallic masthead typography
continues as a provisional visual treatment.

Photos are browser-cropped; credit appears as
Diego Serrano / CC BY 2.0 / cropped for layout.

correct wrestler identity and portrait crop. The normal default production
board is not altered. This preview is not a locked Gold Master.
