three-photo hero when source-backed, ring/arena lighting, verified match
and result panels, ticker and five-show schedule row. The rejected
standalone checkerboard master logo is never used.

Results & Fallout takes only manually reviewed, previous completed
episode results and storyline developments through the exact-date-gated
editorialRecap(show,key) function. October 16, 2026's SmackDown includes
five reviewed match outcomes and three documented developments.
The board cycles through these short paraphrased source facts.
Photographs are used only if a wrestler's name is explicitly present
in the dated recap. No upcoming match images are implied to be from
the finished show.

The next completed show's missing recap produces an update fallback,
NEVER stale winners or fabricated championship holders.
