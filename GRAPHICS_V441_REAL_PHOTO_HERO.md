# V4.41: sourced real-wrestler editorial hero (no AI images)

The visual reference features a three-wrestler hero. This standalone
preview brings the EXACT three names in that reference into the finished
broadcast board using REAL licensed photographs, not image generation.

New archival photos from Royal Rumble 2025:
- LA Knight: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LA_Knight_RR25.jpg
- Roman Reigns: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roman_Reigns_RR25_(1).jpg
- Cody Rhodes: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cody_Rhodes_RR25.jpg

Each is credited to Diego Serrano and listed as CC BY 2.0 on Wikimedia.
The collector validates the exact file identity and license metadata
before caching a locally hosted JPEG. There is no browser hotlink.

The editorial photo fallback is used only on the Upcoming SmackDown page
when fewer than two photographs match actual named competitors in the
current date-matched source-reported card. The hero explicitly labels
itself as archive imagery, NOT advertised appearances. When two or more
source-reported contestants match our photo library, their images
take priority.

The Results & Fallout page may use only people who actually appear
by name in that completed episode's reviewed recap text; it does
not reuse archival hero images when no results are available.
