# AAA Heroes Inmortales: exact TV-week card map (Oct 23, 2026 review)

**Reason for this change:** generic articles often identify the five-match
Héroes Inmortales event card without specifying which Saturday television
episode actually includes each match. Publishing all five as October 24
would misrepresent the three-week broadcast.

Source-reviewed, date-scoped records:

- October 24, Week 1: El Grande Americano vs Dominik Mysterio (Mega title);
  La Catalina vs Flammer vs La Hiedra (Reina de Reinas title); Los Perros
  del Mal vs La Parka, Mr. Iguana & Niño Hamburguesa (World Trios title).
  Marisela Peña's Hall of Fame induction is a scheduled segment, not a match.
- October 31, Week 2: Rey Fenix vs Mini Vikingo (Cruiserweight title).
- November 7, Week 3: El Fiscal vs Abismo Negro Jr.

Two independent episode-specific articles explicitly establish this
television week breakdown:
- TPWW, October 17 TV notes:
  https://www.tpww.net/2026/10/10-17-aaa-tv-notes-quick-match-results-el-fiscal-vs-abismo-negro-and-flammer-vs-la-hiedra-vs-la-catalina-for-aaa-reina-de-reinas-title-set-for-aaa-heroes-inmortales-2026/
- 411Mania, October 18 full lineup separated by Week 01/02/03:
  https://411mania.com/wrestling/aaa-heroes-inmortales-2026-full-lineup/

An F4W event-level article lists all bouts under the Oct 24 event date;
**that does not establish the Oct 24 TV episode lineup**, so it is not
used for episode assignment.

The `web/data/aaa-tv-week-cards-20261023.json` file is an opt-in,
source-attributed **manual research supplement**, not an automatically
generated results API. The separate v4.31 preview reads it only when
TVmaze's next airdate **exactly equals** the record's TV date; entries
stop appearing when their episode date passes, is changed or withdrawn.
Never infer a match winner, planned appearance, or title change.

Links, original publisher names, and confidence flags are kept in JSON,
not displayed. Data are short original descriptions of facts, not copied
publisher text. Match replacements/postponements need explicit new review.
Prior approved v4.22/v4.29 and v4.30 previews, as well as the production
index and the unrelated 28 sports boards, remain untouched.
