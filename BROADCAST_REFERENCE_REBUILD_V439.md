# V4.39 — SmackDown actual broadcast composition

Review: https://broncos5523.github.io/Inside-the-squared-circle/preview-v4-39-reference-broadcast.html?show=smackdown&view=broadcast

This is a working HTML/CSS/JS preview—not a generated mockup—built
toward the density and hierarchy of the user's original full-board SmackDown
reference. It only activates for show=smackdown and view=broadcast.
V4.38, the public production index and all other shows remain unchanged.

From top to bottom: metallic Man Cave text mark and original tagline
(provisional master artwork, not a locked logo); real official show marks on
both sides; a large arena-lit layered hero from exactly matched real wrestler
photographs; validated next-broadcast date and venue strip; an actual
rotating reported featured matchup with stipulation; other reported matches;
explicitly advertised appearances OR date-labelled last-week storyline notes;
five-show schedule; and a source-grounded bottom ticker.

Only real-source-gated card competitors with exact image allowlist matches
can be featured in the hero. Real 2025 Diego Serrano photographs are
software-masked (not cutout files) under Creative Commons BY 2.0. Ring
managers and fictional wrestler likenesses are not silently added.

Important: source photos and metadata are not current champions or promised
appearances; the reference image's fictional results and venue are never used.
No standings, rankings, fake title belts, AI images, or publisher article
links appear. WCN private cache remains out of public GitHub Pages.
